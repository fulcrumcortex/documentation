Fulcrum user guides

Step-by-step guides for the apps behind Fulcrum Cortex: Fulcrum Mail workspaces, Calendar and booking pages, the Lyre voice agent and Fulcrum CRM.

Fulcrum Cortex is the AI execution layer for business. It runs a team of specialised AI agents next to your people, and you direct them from the chat apps you already use. These guides cover the day-to-day apps that sit underneath it: your mail and calendar, the pages you share with customers, the Lyre voice agent and the CRM. Each guide sticks to what the apps do today and uses the same button names you will see on screen.

Choose a product

Fulcrum MailAccounts, company workspaces, members, and everyday email. CalendarEvents, invitations, connected calendars and video meetings. PagesBooking pages and other links you can share with people outside your team. LyreVoice agents that answer, call, qualify and book. Fulcrum CRMContacts, companies, deals, pipelines and lead capture.

How the pieces fit together

Each app works on its own, and they share one sign-in. A free Fulcrum Mail address (an "Fmail" account) is the identity behind the other apps. You use it to sign in to Lyre, for example. The Fulcrum Mail guides cover that account.

  • Fulcrum Mail holds your email, Calendar, Meetings, Drive and Wallet, grouped into personal and company workspaces.
  • Booking pages come from Calendar. A booking page is a public link that lets anyone pick a free time with you.
  • Lyre is the voice layer. Its agents answer inbound calls, run outbound call lists and can book meetings.
  • Fulcrum CRM stores your contacts, companies and deals. It logs calls, emails and meetings against each record.

The Cortex platform overview describes how the agents are coordinated across these apps. The Cortex plans and pricing page shows what each subscription includes.

Where to start

  1. Create a free Fulcrum Mail address, or sign in with the one you have. See Create an account and sign in.
  2. If you work with a team, set up a company workspace on your own domain and invite your colleagues.
  3. Publish a booking page so customers can book time without back-and-forth email.
  4. Add a Lyre voice agent to answer calls, or bring your contacts into Fulcrum CRM.

Getting help

If something on screen doesn't match these guides, use the correction link at the bottom of the page. The Lyre developer API has its own reference at docs.lyre.au. To see the whole platform working on your own business, book a Fulcrum Cortex demo.
