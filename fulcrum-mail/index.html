Fulcrum Mail

What Fulcrum Mail includes, how its apps are laid out, and where to find guides for accounts, company workspaces, members and everyday email.

Fulcrum Mail is the email and workspace suite at fulcrumail.app. A free account gives you an @fulcrumail.app address and 5 GB of storage shared between Mail and Drive. A company workspace adds mail on your own domain, a team, and shared administration. The same account signs you in to the other Fulcrum apps.

The apps in the left rail

Once you sign in, a rail on the left switches between the apps in your workspace:

AppShown asWhat it's for
FmailConversationsYour inbox, folders, labels, search and filters. See Mail basics.
CalendarEvents & schedulesEvents, invitations and booking pages. See the Calendar guide.
MeetingsVideo conversationsBrowser video meetings with transcripts. See Fulcrum Meetings.
DriveFiles & foldersFiles, company drives and share links. See Pages for public file links.
WorkspacePeople & settingsPeople, groups, domains and workspace settings. Owners and administrators manage these.
DeveloperApps & APIsRegistered apps and personal API tokens, for developers.
WalletCredits & billingYour credit balance, subscription and top-ups.

The Apps button opens Explore Fulcrum apps, which lists the other Fulcrum products you can open with the same sign-in, including Docs, CRM, Lyre, Code and Cortex. The public Fulcrum apps directory lists the same products.

Wallet and credits

Some Fulcrum services are paid with credits from your Wallet. The Wallet shows your balance as credit for the account, at 1 AUD = 1,000 credits, GST included. Its tabs are Overview, Subscription, Top up, Connected apps, Credit lots and History. Workspace guests don't see the Wallet.
